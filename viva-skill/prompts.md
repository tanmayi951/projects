# Viva Practice Skill · Prompt log

1 Turn a good one-off viva practice into a reusable skill. Ask one question at a time, wait for the answer, score it fairly, and cite the user's supplied notes when possible.
2 Keep the examiner grounded: distinguish supported details from missing evidence, accept equivalent wording, and never invent page references or facts absent from the notes.
3 Finish with an overall score, strengths, one revision priority and a short study plan. Include a safe sample note and an instruction for trying the skill in a fresh session.
